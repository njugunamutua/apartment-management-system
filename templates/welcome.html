About Us

Our Vision

To develop an Online Apartment Rental Management System that allows users to rent an apartment without physically contacting the rental company.

Our Mission

To produce a web-based system that allows customers to register and reserve apartments online, and lets the company manage its rental business effectively. To turn the manual process of renting an apartment into an online, computerised one that eases the customer's task whenever they need a home.
